Slumber Harbor & Bramble Bear Songs

This is the home page of two YouTube channels and the small publishing tool behind them.

  • Slumber Harbor — sleep sounds, white noise and lullabies for babies and grown-ups.
  • Bramble Bear Songs — songs, stories and gentle learning videos for young children (made for kids).

The publishing tool

An internal tool used only by the channels' owner. It prepares videos, uploads them to these two channels through the YouTube Data API, schedules them, and reads the channels' own statistics. It is not offered to anyone else and does not sign in any other user.
