Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

This policy covers the internal publishing tool for the YouTube channels Slumber Harbor and Bramble Bear Songs.

Who uses the tool

Only the owner of the two channels. The tool signs in to the owner's own Google account, once per channel, with Google's OAuth consent. It does not sign in, or collect data from, anyone else.

What the tool does with Google data

  • Uploads videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's two channels, and sets their publishing time.
  • Reads the channels' own statistics (views, watch time, subscribers) to plan future videos.

The tool keeps its sign-in token as an encrypted secret that only the owner controls. It does not sell, share or transfer any data, and does not use it for advertising.

Viewers

The tool collects no information about viewers. Comments are off on the children's channel. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to anyone watching the channels on YouTube.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
